Project

RAG Security Assistant

A local RAG pipeline over OWASP and NIST security documents, built with ChromaDB, Ollama, and Llama 3.2 3B. I evaluated retrieval, attacked it with prompt injection, defended it, and published the limitations next to the numbers.

Retrieval
Tested on 18 questions I checked against the source text. The right document appeared in the top 4 results 94% of the time with English embeddings and 100% with multilingual embeddings. That is a one-question difference, so I treat it as a signal, not proof.
Attack
One poisoned document with one injected sentence. With a naive prompt, the model repeated the injected domain in 4 of 5 runs. A hardened prompt did not clearly help (3 of 5).
Defense
An output filter with a domain allowlist blocked 3 of 10 responses that mentioned the unapproved domain. It only covers this kind of attack, and it blocks the whole answer.
Agents
A free tool-calling agent produced 0 of 10 valid reports. A plan-and-execute version that validates citations produced valid reports in 9 of 10 and 7 of 10 runs. Retries did most of the work: in the second run, 3 of 10 were valid on the first attempt.

These are small samples from a single 3B model. The README lists every limitation.
